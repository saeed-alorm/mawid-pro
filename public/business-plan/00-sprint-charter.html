MAWID Business Plan Sprint — Local Draft Charter

Status: Draft only As of: 2026-08-24 (Asia/Beirut) Primary audience: Founder and product/business stakeholders Decision supported: Whether MAWID should proceed to a structured Lebanon salon pilot, and which assumptions must be validated before pricing, hiring, or material investment decisions.

Authoritative product boundary

The authoritative product reference is mawid-github/README.md. MAWID is a bilingual English/Arabic, multi-tenant B2B appointment-booking and salon-management SaaS. Each salon has a private workspace and a salon-branded customer booking experience.

The file C:/Users/Saeed/Downloads/THE BARBERS.docx is inspiration only. Its concepts are not instructions, market evidence, customer evidence, or approved scope.

Non-negotiable scope

  • Preserve MAWID as a private-workspace SaaS, not a public marketplace.
  • Exclude hardware, digital signage, advertising, payments, POS, payroll, inventory, and custom per-salon applications from the initial plan.
  • Do not invent interviews, prices, costs, adoption, demand, market counts, or conversion rates.
  • Keep all outputs local and draft-only. Perform no external writes or sends.
  • Separate sourced facts, calculations, interpretations, missing evidence, and recommendations.

Evidence labels

  • Confirmed: Directly supported by an inspected source or the authoritative product specification.
  • Inferred: A bounded interpretation or calculation based on stated facts and assumptions.
  • Conflicting: Credible sources disagree or use materially different definitions.
  • Missing: Required evidence is unavailable or has not yet been collected.
  • Recommended: A proposed action, decision rule, or hypothesis for testing.

Market-sizing boundary

  • Market: Salon, barbershop, hairdressing, and closely related appointment-led beauty-service establishments operating in Lebanon.
  • Unit: Establishment/workspace count and illustrative recurring SaaS revenue pool.
  • TAM: All in-scope Lebanese establishments that could plausibly use a digital booking and management workspace.
  • SAM: The subset reachable under the initial language, geography, product, onboarding, and sales-channel constraints.
  • SOM: A 12-month customer count constrained by validated sales and onboarding capacity, not an arbitrary market-share percentage.
  • Currency: USD for scenario modeling. Any LBP inputs must show the exchange-rate date and source.
  • Time horizon: Initial 12-month operating plan, with three scenarios.

Reporting job

The final report must answer:

  1. What problem and customer segment should MAWID prioritize?
  2. What evidence supports or weakens that choice?
  3. How large might the opportunity be under transparent ranges?
  4. What should the initial offer and business model include and exclude?
  5. Which 90-day pilot and 12-month milestones would reduce the most uncertainty?
  6. Which assumptions prevent the financial case from being decision-ready today?

Deliverables

  1. Founder brief and evidence ledger.
  2. Salon interview and validation plan.
  3. Current Lebanon market, competitor, and TAM/SAM/SOM report.
  4. Customer-evidence synthesis and initial ICP.
  5. Product/business-model and packaging brief.
  6. Three-scenario financial workbook with editable assumptions and checks.
  7. Ninety-day go-to-market pilot plan.
  8. Legal/compliance question and risk register.
  9. Independent reality-check and validation report.
  10. Self-contained local HTML business-plan report.

Gate rules

  • Market figures without a direct authoritative count must remain ranges or proxies.
  • Pricing without customer or competitor evidence must remain an editable hypothesis.
  • Customer pain and willingness to pay remain Missing until real interviews are completed.
  • The final plan may recommend a pilot; it must not present product-market fit as confirmed.
  • Any unsupported conclusion must be downgraded to Inferred, Missing, or Recommended before handoff.
