Executive Summary: MAWID Evidence-Gated Lebanon Plan

1. SITUATION OVERVIEW

Confirmed: MAWID is a fictional, browser-local bilingual private-workspace SaaS concept, not a public marketplace. Missing: A legally cleared, customer-validated, production-ready service has no supporting customer, commercial, technical, or legal-clearance evidence. Recommended: Use this plan for local decisions and evidence collection only.

2. KEY FINDINGS

Missing: There are 0 customer interviews and 0 validated ICPs. Three cohorts are Recommended for learning: independent owner-managed salons, multi-barber shops, and multi-service women’s hair-and-beauty salons. Strategic implication: no segment, pain, buyer, willingness-to-pay, or demand claim is decision-ready.

Inferred: The only market screen is a 5,369.65 low-confidence establishment-universe proxy from Jordan 2018 ISIC 9602 and current WDI population data. The 4,027.24–6,712.06 band is Recommended sensitivity, not TAM or SAM; both are Missing. Strategic implication: account-list and interview evidence must replace top-down sizing.

Confirmed: Three official competitor price benchmarks were captured, but Lebanon availability and Arabic admin, customer, and RTL support are Missing for all three. Strategic implication: competitor prices cannot become MAWID price or willingness-to-pay evidence.

Confirmed: Financial controls passed 12/12 QA cases and suppress outputs when evidence is incomplete. Six, 12, and 24 workspaces are Recommended month-12 delivery-capacity cases. Strategic implication: no financial forecast, break-even, cash need, or investment conclusion is valid today.

Confirmed: Six external-pilot legal gate conditions remain unresolved, including entity/tax, data-flow/role analysis, communications, security, consumer terms, and customer/workflow evidence. Strategic implication: external use remains blocked.

3. BUSINESS IMPACT

Missing: Revenue, cost, ROI, CAC, market share, and probability of success cannot be quantified. Confirmed: The plan spans 90 days and 13 weeks without a commercial outcome claim. Recommended: Avoid treating a prototype, competitor context, or capacity case as evidence for pricing, hiring, investment, charging, or launch.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

Critical — Recommended: Founder and Legal engage qualified Lebanese legal, tax, and security reviewers within 30 days; expected result: disposition of all 6 external-pilot gates.

High — Recommended: Founder and Discovery prepare the 90-day research package for separate authorization after the legal/research gate; expected result: a permissioned, de-identified method.

High — Recommended: Product and Engineering document production/data-flow and tenant-control requirements within 30 days; expected result: a readiness-gap register without processing real data.

Medium — Recommended: Finance maintain the 12-month blocked-input workbook; expected result: 6/12/24 capacity remains separate from price, cost, demand, and forecast claims.

5. NEXT STEPS

  1. Recommended: Founder confirms the local-only evidence owner and legal-review scope by 2026-09-24.
  2. Recommended: Legal, Security, Product, and Finance log gate evidence and blockers by 2026-09-24.

Recommended decision point: By 2026-09-24, retain NO-GO for external pilot, launch, pricing, hiring, financial forecast, investment, charging, real data, and external communications; GO only for local planning, qualified review, and separately authorized discovery after the legal gate.
