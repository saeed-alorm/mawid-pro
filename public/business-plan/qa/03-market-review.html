SUPERSEDED — DO NOT USE: Initial prioritized findings and exact fixes (remediated)

P0 — Required current competitor and pricing research is missing

Confirmed: The market report contains no named competitor, current official product page, current official pricing page, price basis, currency/locale, source capture date, Lebanon-availability result, or Arabic/RTL result. This fails the sprint requirement for current, cited competitor and pricing work. It is not cured by labelling the entire section Missing.

Inferred impact: Product packaging, pricing hypotheses, differentiation, and financial-model inputs would otherwise be formed without the required external benchmark. Public prices from other locales are not evidence of Lebanon availability, Lebanon willingness to pay, or a viable MAWID price.

Recommended exact fix: Add a dated, named comparison using current official vendor pages only. At minimum, compare three relevant alternatives and separate overlapping MAWID capabilities from out-of-scope marketplace, payments, POS, payroll, marketing, or hardware features. The table must include official URL, capture date, intended segment, booking/calendar/staff/client-management overlap, price and billing unit exactly as displayed, display locale/currency, marketplace model, and separately labelled Lebanon availability and Arabic/RTL evidence. Suitable current primary starting points verified in this review are:

  • Fresha official pricing: <https://www.fresha.com/pricing> — currently displays subscription pricing by independent/team member and includes marketplace/payment/other adjacent features; treat the displayed EUR values as a locale-specific benchmark only.
  • Booksy official US pricing: <https://biz.booksy.com/pricing> — currently displays a base subscription plus added-user price and includes a marketplace; treat USD/US packaging as a locale-specific benchmark only.
  • SimplyBook.me official pricing: <https://simplybook.me/en/pricing> — currently displays plan, booking-volume, and provider limits; record the actual locale/currency returned at capture.

Keep Lebanon availability, Arabic admin UI, Arabic customer UI, and full RTL as Missing unless an official page directly verifies each point. Do not convert competitor list prices into MAWID willingness-to-pay evidence.

P0 — Current World Bank values do not match the saved inputs

Confirmed: As of this review, the World Bank World Development Indicators table for SP.POP.TOTL reports 2018 population values of 5,984,119 for Lebanon and 10,462,306 for Jordan. The JSON instead stores 6,848,925 and 10,101,694 while describing them as World Bank-series inputs. Current primary reference: <https://databank.worldbank.org/reports.aspx?country=LBN%2CJOR&series=SP.POP.TOTL&source=2>.

Confirmed calculation spot-check: The saved arithmetic is internally consistent with the saved values: 9,388 × 6,848,925 ÷ 10,101,694 = 6,365.04; applying 75%–125% and then 162/250 reproduces the JSON outputs. It is nevertheless source-stale. With the current WDI values, the same formula gives a central value of 5,369.65, a 75%–125% envelope of 4,027.24–6,712.06, and—if the otherwise invalid OSM share were applied—2,609.65–4,349.42.

Inferred impact: The reported central proxy is about 18.5% above the same calculation using the current WDI vintage; all TAM/SAM endpoints are affected. WDI revisions also demonstrate why an access/capture date and exact vintage must be saved.

Recommended exact fix: Refresh both countries from the same current WDI source and year, save both exact URLs and access date, replace the JSON values, and regenerate every derived value. If an archived earlier WDI vintage is intentionally used, cite that archive explicitly and do not call it the current World Bank series.

P0 — OSM evidence is not reproducible and cannot support SAM

Confirmed: The report calls the OSM observation “reproducible” and says its query definition is recorded. The JSON instead says: “The exact raw query and result export were not retained.” It contains only the OSM homepage, prose about an unspecified tag set, and two numbers. It does not preserve Overpass QL, tag values, area IDs or administrative boundary rules, query time, raw output, deduplication, operational-status handling, or parsing/count code.

Inferred impact: Neither 250 nor 162 can be independently verified. Because duplicates and closed/stale features may exist, these figures are not guaranteed lower bounds on unique operating establishments. The 162/250 share may measure mapping coverage rather than business distribution, so it cannot currently support SAM.

Recommended exact fix: Either remove the OSM counts and all SAM calculations that depend on 0.648, or save and rerun an auditable package containing exact Overpass QL, included/excluded tags, Lebanon and Beirut/Mount Lebanon boundary identifiers, UTC timestamp, raw JSON/CSV result, unique-key rule (osm_type + osm_id), duplicate/closed-place handling, and deterministic count code. Until that exists, label the counts and geographic share Missing, not Confirmed.

P1 — The Jordan primary source exists and should replace the “missing URL” claim

Confirmed: Jordan Department of Statistics, Economic Establishments Census 2018, Table 18 reports 9,388 active establishments for ISIC 9602 (“Hairdressing and other beauty treatment”): <https://dosweb.dos.gov.jo/DataBank/Census_Establishment2018/table18.pdf>. Table 38 reports 16,607 employees for the same activity: <https://dosweb.dos.gov.jo/DataBank/Census_Establishment2018/En/table38.pdf>. The census landing/table index is <https://dosweb.dos.gov.jo/censuses/establish-census/establish-census-2018/establish_tables/>.

Inferred impact: The report and JSON incorrectly leave the exact Jordan source URL Missing, despite the user requiring current source-backed work. The worker count is unused in the model and should either be removed or explicitly used only as a reasonableness check.

Recommended exact fix: Add Table 18 as the direct establishment source, Table 38 only if the employee figure is retained, and record the source year, activity definition, unit (active establishment), and coverage. Keep the cross-country transfer itself Inferred.

P1 — The proxy is an establishment-universe screen, not a defensible TAM/SAM estimate

Inferred: Jordan ISIC 9602 scaled by total population does not establish how many Lebanese businesses are appointment-led, digitally reachable, operationally compatible, willing to adopt, or economically serviceable. The 75%–125% factor is explicitly judgmental but has no empirical basis, and the resulting endpoints are not confidence bounds. Applying an unverified OSM share compounds rather than resolves the uncertainty.

Inferred impact: Calling 4,774–7,956 “TAM” and 3,093–5,156 “SAM” can give a quick reader false precision even with adjacent caveats. The charter’s TAM definition requires plausible product use, while SAM requires reachable language, geography, onboarding, and channel constraints that are not measured here.

Recommended exact fix: Rename the population-scaled output “low-confidence establishment-universe proxy.” Show the formula and arbitrary sensitivity as Recommended scenario assumptions, not confidence intervals. Keep TAM and SAM Missing until a Lebanon source or documented target-account frame supplies eligibility and geography. If a numeric proxy remains, place the uncertainty in the headline itself and do not chart it as a measured market range.

Confirmed: Several labels do not meet the sprint definitions:

  • An OSM count without the query/result artifact is not Confirmed.

Recommended exact fix: Re-label each material statement at claim level after source repair. Use Conflicting only for genuine source disagreement; use Missing for unverified facts and Inferred for bounded interpretations.

Update the report and handoff exclusively from those corrected inputs.
