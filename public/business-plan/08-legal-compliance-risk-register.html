MAWID Lebanon Legal and Compliance Issue-Spotting Register

Status: Local draft only; issue spotting, not legal advice or a compliance opinion As of / source access date: 2026-08-25 (Asia/Beirut) Decision use: Identify questions and gates before external outreach, real-data collection, contracting, or an external pilot.

Executive gate

Confirmed: MAWID is currently a fictional, browser-local prototype using fictional records, local state, simulated OTP, and no real authentication, SMS, payments, or customer data. Source: mawid-github/README.md.

Confirmed: The current prototype is not evidence that an external service is legally, operationally, or security ready.

Missing: MAWID’s legal entity, contracting model, data flows, processing roles, hosting/vendor locations, user age profile, notification provider, tax position, terms, privacy notice, security controls, incident procedure, and production architecture are unknown.

Recommended: Do not send outreach, collect or process real appointment/contact data, use real SMS/email/communications providers, contract with salons, charge fees, or activate an external pilot until qualified Lebanese counsel and relevant tax/accounting/technical owners have reviewed the evidence items below.

Scope and advice limitations

Confirmed: This register preserves MAWID as a private-workspace SaaS and excludes marketplace, hardware/signage, advertising, payments, POS, payroll, inventory, and custom salon applications from the initial plan.

Missing: This register is not a legal opinion, does not determine compliance, does not interpret all applicable laws/implementing measures, and does not authorize outreach, data processing, contracting, or launch. It does not replace advice from qualified counsel, a tax adviser, or a security professional.

Recommended: Recheck every primary source and obtain qualified Lebanese advice immediately before any external action, because legal/regulatory status, implementing practice, and business facts can change.
