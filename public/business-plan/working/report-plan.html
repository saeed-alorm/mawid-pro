MAWID Executive Report Plan

Reporting job

  • Question: Should MAWID proceed to a structured Lebanon salon pilot, and what evidence, offer, economics, risks, and milestones should govern that decision?
  • Audience: Product stakeholders (founder and business/product decision-makers).
  • Scope: Lebanon; private-workspace bilingual salon SaaS; initial 12-month operating plan.
  • Comparison basis: Low/base/high market ranges and downside/base/upside operating scenarios.
  • Success criterion: A reader can distinguish what is known, assumed, missing, and recommended and can decide whether to fund a limited validation pilot without mistaking projections for demand evidence.
  • Delivery mode: One self-contained local HTML report, generated from a canonical artifact.json. Supporting Markdown and the financial workbook remain audit artifacts, not parallel report surfaces.

Evidence path

  1. Product scope and concept-note comparison.
  2. Market definition, source quality, bottom-up calculation chain, range and sensitivity.
  3. Competitor and substitute comparison using official/current sources where accessible.
  4. Customer discovery status, initial segment hypotheses, and explicit absence of interviews.
  5. Product/business-model recommendation and non-goals.
  6. Formula-driven three-scenario operating model with editable assumptions.
  7. Ninety-day GTM pilot and decision gates.
  8. Legal/compliance risks and questions requiring qualified Lebanese counsel.
  9. Reality-check verdict and required caveats.

Visual omissions

  • No customer-demand, pain-frequency, willingness-to-pay, CAC, retention, or conversion chart will be shown because no real observations exist.
  • Competitor evidence will use a table because exact feature/source lookup is more useful than a chart.
  • Legal and operational risks will use a ranked table because severity is qualitative and should not be disguised as precision.
