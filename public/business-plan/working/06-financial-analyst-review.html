MAWID Stage 6B — Independent Financial Analyst Review

Status: Local draft only As of: 2026-08-25 (Asia/Beirut) Decision use: Define and challenge a 12-month three-scenario operating model; not a forecast, valuation, funding recommendation, price decision, or launch authorization. Independence: Confirmed: This assessment was completed before reading any FP&A stage-6 draft.

Financial conclusion

Confirmed: The current evidence supports a formula-driven financial-model structure, but it does not support populated commercial forecasts. The only approved scenario quantities are 6 / 12 / 24 active workspaces at month 12, labelled Recommended delivery-capacity cases rather than demand, conversion, SOM, or market share.

Missing: MAWID price, packaging basis, billing currency, activation ramp, churn, collection timing, onboarding effort, production technology costs, notification costs, support burden, sales cost, founder compensation treatment, headcount plan, legal/accounting cost, tax treatment, starting cash, minimum cash buffer, and committed financing.

Recommended: Build the workbook as an auditable scenario engine with blank required commercial inputs and a visible status of BLOCKED — MISSING INPUTS. Formulas may calculate only after every required input has a value, evidence label, source or assumption owner, and as-of date. A founder-approved illustrative input remains Recommended, not Confirmed, and its output remains a scenario rather than a forecast.

Required model architecture

Recommended: Use a monthly January-to-December-style 12-period axis labelled Month 1 through Month 12 until an approved start date exists. Do not imply calendar timing without a confirmed launch/pilot date.

  1. Recommended — Cover / Decision Summary: purpose, version, as-of date, scenario selector, evidence warning, model status, key outputs and stop conditions.
  2. Recommended — Assumptions: three scenario columns; each row includes input, unit, label, source/owner, as-of date, and promotion condition. Blank must remain distinct from zero.
  3. Recommended — Operating Drivers: opening workspaces, activations, churned workspaces, ending workspaces, billable workspace-months, onboarding hours, support usage and production-service units.
  4. Recommended — Forecast Model: recognized revenue, cash receipts, direct costs, gross contribution, fixed operating cash costs, operating result, cash disbursements, financing and ending cash.
  5. Recommended — Scenarios / Sensitivities: side-by-side downside/base/upside outputs and formula-driven one-/two-way sensitivities.
  6. Recommended — Checks: one assertion per row with Actual, Expected, Difference, Tolerance, Status and fix location.
  7. Recommended — Sources / Audit: exact source or assumption owner, version history, retrieval/approval date, notes and scope exclusions.

Recommended: Do not build a DCF, valuation, LTV, CAC payback, tax-adjusted return, or probabilistic forecast. The inputs required for those outputs are Missing, and false precision would exceed the sprint decision.

Core formulas and guards

Recommended: Use transparent helper rows and named assumptions; do not place numeric constants inside calculation formulas.

  • EndingWorkspaces_t = OpeningWorkspaces_t + Activations_t - ChurnedWorkspaces_t
  • OpeningWorkspaces_t = EndingWorkspaces_(t-1)
  • AverageActiveWorkspaces_t = (OpeningWorkspaces_t + EndingWorkspaces_t) / 2 only if billing-day detail is unavailable and the simplification is explicitly Recommended.
  • RecurringRevenue_t = BillableWorkspaceMonths_t × RecurringPrice_t
  • RecognizedRevenue_t = RecurringRevenue_t + ApprovedSetupRevenue_t - DiscountsAndCredits_t
  • CashReceipts_t = CollectionsFromCurrentAndPriorBillings_t; do not equate receipts with revenue unless timing is explicitly approved.
  • DirectServiceCost_t = ActiveUsageUnits_t × UnitCost_t + ActiveWorkspaces_t × DirectCostPerWorkspace_t
  • OnboardingCashCost_t = Activations_t × OnboardingHoursPerActivation_t × LoadedHourlyCost_t
  • GrossContribution_t = RecognizedRevenue_t - DirectServiceCost_t - OnboardingCostClassifiedAsDirect_t
  • OperatingResult_t = GrossContribution_t - FixedOperatingExpense_t
  • EndingCash_t = OpeningCash_t + CashReceipts_t + FinancingInflows_t - CashDisbursements_t
  • CashNeed = MAX(0, MinimumCashBuffer - MIN(EndingCashBeforeNewFinancing_t))

Recommended: Guard all divisions and decision outputs. When a required input is blank, display N/A — Missing input; when contribution per workspace is non-positive, display No break-even under current assumptions rather than an error or negative workspace count.

Break-even and liquidity logic

Recommended: Distinguish three concepts:

  1. Recommended — recurring operating break-even: FixedRecurringCashCost / RecurringContributionPerWorkspaceMonth, only when recurring contribution is positive. This excludes one-time onboarding and financing.
  2. Recommended — monthly cash break-even: first month in which operating cash receipts are at least operating cash disbursements, shown only after collection/payment timing is populated.
  3. Recommended — cumulative cash recovery: first month cumulative cash flow returns to the starting-cash level after setup and build costs; it may not occur within 12 months.

Missing: None of these can be calculated credibly today. CashNeed, lowest cash month, runway, and funding gap must remain N/A until opening cash, minimum buffer, receipts timing and all material cash costs are supplied.

Recommended: Record production build/readiness cash separately from recurring operations. The browser-local prototype is Confirmed as a prototype, but production architecture, security, tenant isolation, authentication, notifications, privacy controls, support and legal readiness costs are Missing.

Sensitivity priorities

Recommended: Sensitivities must alter underlying drivers and recalculate monthly cash; do not paste static outputs.

  1. Recommended: active-workspace ramp × recurring price → year-one revenue, ending MRR and cash need.
  2. Recommended: recurring price × direct cost per workspace → contribution and break-even workspaces.
  3. Recommended: onboarding hours × loaded labor cost → onboarding cash burden and capacity.
  4. Recommended: fixed-cost start month × monthly fixed cash cost → minimum cash and lowest-cash month.
  5. Recommended: churned workspaces × activations → ending active workspaces and replacement workload.
  6. Recommended: collection delay × starting cash/minimum buffer → runway and financing need.
  7. Recommended: USD/LBP path × LBP-denominated cost base → USD reporting result and cash need.

Missing: No numeric sensitivity bands are justified. The workbook should accept user-approved low/base/high inputs rather than inventing percentage shocks.

Currency and tax caveats

Confirmed: The sprint charter selects USD as the model reporting currency.

Missing: Billing currency, expense currencies, bank/collection mechanics, an authoritative FX source/vintage, and the proportion of costs exposed to LBP or other currencies.

Recommended: Store every cash input in transaction currency with a monthly FX rate and source; translate to USD in a visible helper schedule. Do not reuse one undated rate or assume USD billing eliminates local-currency exposure. Include an FX sensitivity only after the cost-currency mix is defined.

Missing: Lebanese corporate income tax, VAT, payroll/social contributions, registration, invoicing, withholding, and cross-border/vendor tax treatment have not been established for MAWID.

Recommended: Present pre-tax operating scenarios and a visibly blocked tax section until a qualified Lebanese accountant or lawyer confirms entity, invoicing, payroll and tax facts. Do not use competitor tax disclosures as MAWID tax inputs.

Evidence gates and stop conditions

  • Recommended — structural model gate: proceed now with formulas, empty inputs, checks and sources. Do not show commercial outputs as numbers when required inputs are blank.
  • Recommended — illustrative scenario gate: populate only after the founder explicitly approves each assumption, owner and date. Keep outputs labelled illustrative and do not call the base case likely.
  • Missing — commercial decision gate: no MAWID price, revenue forecast, CAC, LTV, payback, break-even or funding recommendation is evidence-backed until customer, funnel, cost and collection evidence exists.
  • Missing — launch/hiring gate: do not use the workbook to authorize production launch or hiring until production readiness, legal/tax, support, onboarding and cash inputs are reviewed.
  • Recommended — update gate: replace assumptions with actuals monthly during any later approved pilot; preserve version history and forecast-versus-actual definitions.

Independent decision

Recommended: Approve construction of a blocked, formula-complete three-scenario workbook. Do not approve a populated base-case forecast from current evidence. The decision-useful result today is the ranked validation agenda and visibility into which inputs control cash survival—not a revenue or valuation number.
