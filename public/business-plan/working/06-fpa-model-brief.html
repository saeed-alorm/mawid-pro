MAWID FP&A Model Design Brief — Evidence-Gated 12-Month Workbook

Status: Local draft only As of: 2026-08-25 (Asia/Beirut) Decision use: A formula-driven planning shell for a narrow pilot. It must not present revenue, cost, cash need, profitability, or break-even as known outcomes until the required inputs are observed or independently sourced.

Planning posture and boundary

Confirmed: MAWID is a private-workspace B2B SaaS prototype with bilingual booking, approval/rescheduling, employee schedules, and management workflows. Marketplace, hardware/signage, advertising, payments, POS, payroll, inventory, and custom per-salon applications remain excluded. Sources: mawid-github/README.md; 05-product-offer-and-packaging.md.

Missing: MAWID price, paid conversion, activation, churn/retention, payment collection timing, tax treatment, payment-processing use, hosting cost, message cost, support/onboarding time, founder compensation, salaries, vendors, marketing spend, legal spend, and opening cash are not evidenced.

Recommended: Build a 12-month, monthly workbook with three editable capacity cases: downside 6, base 12, upside 24 active workspaces by month 12. These are capacity cases, not a forecast of demand, sales conversion, TAM/SAM, or market share. Source: working/market-inputs.json.

Formula design

Capacity ramp

For each scenario s and month m:

Beginning Active[s,m] = IF(m=1, Opening Active[s], Ending Active[s,m-1])
Gross Go-Lives[s,m]  = editable monthly input OR formula-driven ramp allocation
Churned[s,m]         = IF(churn input complete, Beginning Active[s,m] × churn rate, INPUT REQUIRED)
Ending Active[s,m]   = Beginning Active[s,m] + Gross Go-Lives[s,m] - Churned[s,m]

Recommended: In the initial blank model, set Opening Active to zero only as a transparent presentation assumption, set monthly go-lives as editable cells that reconcile to the 6 / 12 / 24 endpoints, and do not calculate churn until a definition and input exist. The capacity ramp must never silently assume conversion or retention.

Revenue and contribution

Subscription Revenue[m] = Average Active Workspaces[m] × Monthly MAWID Price / Workspace
Implementation Revenue[m] = Go-Lives[m] × Implementation Fee
Total Revenue[m] = Subscription Revenue[m] + Implementation Revenue[m]
Variable Service Cost[m] = Average Active Workspaces[m] × Variable Cost / Workspace
Contribution[m] = Total Revenue[m] - Variable Service Cost[m]
Contribution Margin[m] = IF(Total Revenue[m]<>0, Contribution[m]/Total Revenue[m], "INPUT REQUIRED")

Missing: Price, implementation fee, and variable service cost prevent a quantified revenue or contribution output today.

Fixed costs, operating result, and cash need

Fixed Operating Cost[m] = SUM(Engineering, Hosting baseline, Support, Sales & Marketing, Legal/Compliance, G&A)
Operating Result[m] = Contribution[m] - Fixed Operating Cost[m]
Closing Cash[m] = Opening Cash[m] + Operating Result[m] + Funding Inflows[m] - Non-P&L Cash Outflows[m]
Maximum Cash Need = MAX(0, -MIN(Closing Cash[1:12]))

Missing: The model cannot quantify operating result or cash need without cost, opening-cash, funding, and cash-timing inputs. It should display INPUT REQUIRED, not a fabricated zero result.

Symbolic break-even

Break-even Workspaces = Fixed Monthly Operating Cost / (Monthly MAWID Price - Variable Cost per Workspace)

Recommended: Show the formula symbolically while price/cost inputs are missing. Return NOT COMPUTABLE if contribution per workspace is blank, zero, or negative. Once complete inputs exist, identify the first month where ending active workspaces equal/exceed calculated break-even workspaces; label that an illustrative model output, not an observed milestone.

Operating cadence after pilot starts

Recommended: Reforecast monthly using actual active workspaces, go-lives, paused/cancelled accounts, cash receipts, variable service consumption, and approved costs. Track forecast-versus-actual variance by driver (capacity, price, churn, variable cost, fixed cost, timing), explain forward impact, and revise scenarios instead of carrying forward an invalid plan.

Handoff constraints

Recommended: The workbook builder may create a usable, auditable blank-input workbook now. It must not insert assumed prices or costs merely to make charts populate. An executive report may show the capacity cases and a list of blocking inputs; it must not show fabricated financial results.
