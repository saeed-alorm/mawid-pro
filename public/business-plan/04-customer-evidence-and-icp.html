MAWID Customer Evidence and Provisional ICP

Status: Local draft only As of: 2026-08-25 (Asia/Beirut) Decision use: Product-discovery gate; not a demand, pricing, or launch decision.

Decision summary

Confirmed: MAWID’s prototype is a bilingual English/Arabic, private-workspace B2B appointment-booking and salon-management product with a salon-branded customer flow, booking requests that await salon approval, employee schedules, and management workflows. It is not a public marketplace. Source: mawid-github/README.md.

Missing: No salon, barbershop, manager, employee, or end-customer interviews, pilot observations, product analytics, support records, sales calls, or customer quotations have been supplied. No customer finding can therefore be synthesized, counted, scored, or attributed to a segment.

Recommended: Treat the three segments below as provisional learning cohorts, not as an established ideal customer profile (ICP) or evidence of demand. Authorize Product Manager work only to define a narrow test offer and acceptance criteria; do not authorize pricing, a committed roadmap, hiring, or a launch plan until the evidence gate in this document passes.

What is deliberately not synthesized

Missing: There are no respondent records, verbatims, observed appointment events, survey results, reviews, usage data, or support tickets from which to derive themes, sentiment, workflow maps, feature priorities, satisfaction, or segment ranking.

Recommended: Do not infer that salons rely on calls, WhatsApp, paper, spreadsheets, calendar tools, or competing software; do not infer that double-booking, no-shows, staff availability, cancellations, or language friction are material. Each is a neutral discovery topic only.

Recommended: Do not use the fictional salons, bookings, staff, or data in the MAWID demonstration as proxies for real establishments, users, demand, or operating behavior.

Recommended evidence collection and synthesis controls

Recommended: Use the Discovery Coach’s 18-conversation qualitative plan as a learning design, not a demand sample. Preserve respondent ID, segment, role, decision role, broad area, conversation language, concrete event evidence, buying context, constraints, evidence code, specificity, and disconfirming signals.

Recommended: Quarantine notes that contain unnecessary client personal data, full message threads, payment data, or an unsupported interpretation presented as a quotation. Only de-identified, structured records that satisfy the discovery handoff’s acceptance checklist should enter synthesis.

Recommended: After each interview wave, create a respondent-by-theme matrix that counts distinct establishments rather than comments, separates administrative/professional/end-customer needs, shows supporting and counterevidence, and retains Missing cells rather than filling them with assumed practices.
