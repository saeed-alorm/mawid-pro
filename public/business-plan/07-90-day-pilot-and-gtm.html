MAWID 90-Day Discovery-First Pilot and GTM Experiment Plan

Status: Local draft only As of: 2026-08-25 (Asia/Beirut) Decision use: A controlled learning plan for a private-workspace SaaS; not a public launch, demand forecast, sales plan, or customer-acquisition forecast.

Decision posture

Confirmed: MAWID is a browser-local bilingual English/Arabic prototype for private salon workspaces and salon-branded booking flows. The prototype does not provide real authentication, SMS, payments, backend storage, or customer data handling. Source: mawid-github/README.md.

Missing: There are no interviews, leads, customers, pilot participants, channel observations, funnel rates, CAC, conversion, budget, or usage outcomes. There is no customer-validated ICP or price.

Recommended: Use the next 90 days to test the three provisional cohorts with de-identified discovery and concept evaluation before attempting an external pilot. Do not run paid advertising, a marketplace, payment activity, hardware/signage activity, POS, payroll, inventory, or bespoke-app work.

Learning objective and pilot boundary

Recommended: The sole initial learning objective is to determine whether any provisional cohort has a concrete, repeated appointment-management workflow that is compatible with MAWID’s narrow private-workspace concept and its exclusions.

Missing: The following are not established: the pain to solve, prevalence, a preferred booking channel, named-professional importance, approval/reschedule fit, bilingual/RTL value, buyer authority, onboarding burden, willingness to pay, conversion, retention, and unit economics.

Recommended: An external customer pilot may be considered only after customer evidence, product-readiness, security/privacy, legal, support, and operational gates are reviewed. Until then, the “pilot” in this plan means a research and concept-validation program.

Local draft outreach scripts — DO NOT SEND

Recommended: These scripts are local drafts only. Do not send them, place them in an external tool, or use them to collect contact information without separate authorization and Legal review. Make no claims about product availability, price, outcomes, or confidentiality beyond an approved notice.

English — research invitation

DRAFT ONLY — DO NOT SEND Hello [name], I’m researching how salon and barbershop teams currently handle appointment requests, schedule changes, and staff availability. This is not a sales call and there is no product to buy. Would you be open to a short voluntary conversation about a recent booking or schedule-change process? We would keep only de-identified research notes and you can skip any question. If this is not relevant, no reply is needed.
