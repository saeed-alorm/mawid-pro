Handoff 10 — Executive Report Packaging

Status: Local draft only Audience: Founder and internal product/business stakeholders Primary content: 10-executive-synthesis.md

Mandatory decision banner

Recommended: Place this wording before any chart, model, or narrative: NO-GO for external pilot, launch, pricing, hiring, financial forecast, investment, charging, real-data processing, and external communications. GO only for local planning, qualified Lebanese legal/tax/security review, and separately authorized discovery after the legal/research gate.

Packaging instructions

  1. Confirmed: Preserve MAWID as a fictional browser-local, bilingual private-workspace SaaS concept; do not imply a live service or marketplace.
  2. Missing: Show customer validation, ICP, demand, WTP, MAWID price, TAM/SAM/SOM, conversion, CAC, retention/churn, production readiness, legal clearance, and decision-ready financial outputs as unresolved.
  3. Inferred: If the 5,369.65 figure is shown, call it exactly “low-confidence establishment-universe proxy.” Pair it with the Recommended 4,027.24–6,712.06 sensitivity and state that TAM/SAM remain Missing.
  4. Confirmed: Show the 3 competitor public-price captures solely as locale-specific benchmarks; leave Lebanon availability and Arabic/RTL fields as Missing.
  5. Recommended: Show 6/12/24 only as illustrative delivery-capacity cases and retain the 12/12 finance QA result as a control result, never commercial proof.
  6. Confirmed: Exclude marketplace, hardware/signage, advertising, payments, POS, payroll, inventory, and custom salon applications from the plan.
