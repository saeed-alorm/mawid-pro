Handoff 06B — Independent Financial Analyst

From: Financial Analyst To: Finance-model builder, Reality Checker, Executive Summary Generator Status: Local draft only As of: 2026-08-25

Cleared conclusions

  • Confirmed: A three-scenario model can be built structurally, but the current evidence cannot support a populated commercial forecast.
  • Recommended: Preserve 6 / 12 / 24 only as month-12 active-workspace delivery-capacity endpoints.
  • Missing: Price, package basis, monthly activation ramp, churn, collections, production/direct costs, onboarding/support effort, fixed operating plan, starting cash, buffer, financing, FX and tax treatment.
  • Recommended: The workbook must display BLOCKED — MISSING INPUTS until all required inputs have a label, source or assumption owner, unit and date.

Required workbook behavior

  1. Recommended: Separate assumptions, monthly operating drivers, forecast, scenarios/sensitivities, checks and sources/audit.
  2. Recommended: Keep required unknowns blank; never interpret blank as zero.
  3. Recommended: Suppress revenue, break-even, runway and cash-need conclusions with N/A — Missing input until inputs are complete.
  4. Recommended: Distinguish recognized revenue from receipts, direct service/onboarding cost from fixed operating cash cost, operating break-even from monthly cash break-even, and cash recovery from both.
  5. Recommended: Recalculate sensitivities from underlying drivers; first priorities are price, workspace ramp, churn, onboarding, recurring direct cost, fixed-cost timing, collections and FX.
  6. Recommended: Include visible roll-forward, revenue, cost, cash, break-even, scope, formula-error and source-completeness checks.

Dominant decision risks

Stop conditions

  • Missing: No evidence-backed price, revenue forecast, CAC, LTV, payback, break-even, runway, cash need, funding recommendation or valuation can be reported today.
  • Recommended: If the workbook builder needs any numeric input beyond the approved 6 / 12 / 24 capacity endpoints, stop that calculation path or obtain explicit founder approval to label the value Recommended — illustrative.
  • Recommended: Do not use the workbook to authorize launch, hiring or external financing before technical/production, legal/tax, customer, onboarding/support and cash evidence gates are met.

Acceptance criteria for reconciliation

  • Confirmed: The independent review is working/06-financial-analyst-review.md.
  • Recommended: Reconcile any later FP&A proposal against the missing-input hierarchy and formula checks without erasing disagreements.
  • Recommended: Reality Checker should fail any output that presents competitor pricing, capacity cases or blank-default values as MAWID evidence.
