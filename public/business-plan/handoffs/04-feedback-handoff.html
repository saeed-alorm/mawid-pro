Context

Project: MAWID | موعد — bilingual private-workspace B2B appointment-booking and salon-management SaaS for Lebanon.

Confirmed: The authoritative prototype is browser-local and supports a salon-branded customer flow, pending approval, manager approval/rejection/proposed-time actions, employee schedules, and bilingual English/Arabic UI. It is not a marketplace.

Confirmed: Marketplace, hardware/signage, advertising, payments, POS, payroll, inventory, and custom per-salon applications remain outside the initial plan.

Missing: No direct customer interviews, pilot evidence, product usage, sales evidence, customer quotations, willingness-to-pay data, buying-process evidence, or customer requirement evidence exists. The prior artifacts therefore do not support a validated ICP, customer journey, persona, price, demand, sales motion, or roadmap priority.

Relevant files:

  • outputs/mawid-business-plan-sprint/04-customer-evidence-and-icp.md — full evidence inventory, provisional cohorts, promotion rules, and gate.
  • outputs/mawid-business-plan-sprint/02-discovery-plan.md — approved discovery method and collection controls.
  • outputs/mawid-business-plan-sprint/handoffs/02-discovery-handoff.md — required record format for future synthesis.
  • outputs/mawid-business-plan-sprint/03-market-sizing-and-competition.md — competitor context only; not customer evidence.
  • mawid-github/README.md — authoritative product scope.

Dependencies: Product Manager work must preserve the evidence state and scope boundaries in the preceding artifacts.

Constraints: All work remains local and draft-only. Do not send outreach, contact prospects, collect unnecessary personal data, make external changes, or use THE BARBERS as evidence. Use only Confirmed, Inferred, Conflicting, Missing, or Recommended for material statements.

Deliverable Request

What is needed: A narrow Product Manager brief that converts the confirmed product boundary and Missing evidence into a pilot-testable value proposition, operating offer, packaging hypotheses, success measures, and non-goals—without claiming customer validation or setting a definitive price.

Acceptance criteria:

  • [ ] Confirmed: Preserves private-workspace B2B scope and every initial exclusion.
  • [ ] Missing: Does not present pain, demand, Arabic/RTL preference, buyer roles, price, willingness to pay, market fit, CAC, retention, or customer priority as established.
  • [ ] Recommended: Defines only test hypotheses, success/disqualification criteria, and evidence required to decide among the three provisional cohorts.
  • [ ] Recommended: Keeps any price, capacity, and commercial model variables editable and visibly hypothesis-based.
  • [ ] Confirmed: Separates prototype capability from production readiness and customer value.

Quality Expectations

Must pass: Every material claim carries an allowed evidence label and a source/ref; claims beyond current evidence are explicitly Missing or Recommended.

Evidence required: The Product Manager must cite the customer-evidence gate and identify the discovery record(s) that would be needed before converting a hypothesis into a recommendation.

Handoff to next: Send a product/business-model brief to FP&A, Financial Analyst, Growth Hacker, Legal Compliance Checker, and Reality Checker. Any commercial figures must remain scenario inputs, not validated prices or forecasts.

Go / no-go decision

Recommended: GO only for discovery-oriented product definition.

Missing: NO-GO for an ICP claim, price decision, commercial launch, hiring plan, or financial forecast treated as likely outcome until direct customer evidence is collected under the approved discovery plan.
