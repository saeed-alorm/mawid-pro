Status: Local draft only Prepared: 2026-08-24 (Asia/Beirut) Upstream artifacts: 01-founder-brief.md, 01-evidence-ledger.md

Founder direction carried forward

Recommended: Explore a narrow, structured Lebanon pilot for MAWID as a bilingual private-workspace B2B SaaS for appointment-led salons. The purpose is validation, not a launch, scale decision, or product-market-fit claim.

Downstream agents may rely on

ItemLabelReliance permitted
MAWID's private-workspace, multi-tenant B2B product boundaryConfirmedUse as the base scope for product, customer, market, and GTM analysis.
English/Arabic bilingual and RTL workflow requirement in the prototypeConfirmedTreat as an existing product requirement; do not claim customer demand or adoption from it.
Prototype includes a connected booking-request, approval/reschedule, employee-schedule, and management workflowConfirmedUse as a description of prototype scope only.
Exclusions: marketplace, hardware/signage, advertising, payments, POS, payroll, inventory, custom per-salon applicationsConfirmedPreserve in every proposed offer, pilot, financial scenario, and risk register.

Downstream agents must not assume

  • Any confirmed customer pain, demand, willingness to pay, or price point.
  • That appointment-led salons in the defined target segment value a connected booking, staff-schedule, and operational-visibility workflow enough to change behavior; this is Missing and may be used only as an interview hypothesis.
  • Any market-size count, TAM/SAM/SOM point estimate, or market-share forecast.
  • Any acquisition cost, channel conversion, sales cycle, churn, retention, LTV, onboarding capacity, or unit economics.
  • Any actual interview, pilot, customer, competitor, or regulatory evidence not directly cited in the agent's own output.
  • That the browser-local prototype is a production system, evidence of usage, or evidence of technical feasibility beyond the described prototype.
  • That the three fictional demo salons, their data, or their operational examples represent real businesses or segments.
  • That THE BARBERS is a source of MAWID evidence, approval, or scope. It is inspiration only.
  • Marketplace, hardware/signage, advertising, payments, POS, payroll, or custom per-salon app scope.

Required evidence discipline

  1. Use only these material labels: Confirmed, Inferred, Conflicting, Missing, and Recommended.
  2. Cite the source/ref for each material finding. Separate direct facts from calculations, interpretations, and proposals.
  3. Keep customer pain, willingness to pay, market size, pricing, acquisition cost, churn, and demand Missing unless directly sourced.
  4. Where market evidence lacks an authoritative count, present transparent ranges or proxies, explain definitions and limits, and do not convert them into facts.
  5. Keep pricing as an editable hypothesis until direct customer or competitor evidence supports a defined offer.
  6. Do not invent interviews, quotations, prices, operating costs, adoption, funnel performance, or financial assumptions.
  7. Keep all work local and draft-only; do not send messages or modify external systems.

Escalate before proceeding if

  • A downstream output needs a market count, price, customer claim, financial input, or legal conclusion that lacks a cited source.
  • A proposal would add any excluded feature or service model.
  • A conclusion would imply product-market fit, commercial traction, or production readiness without direct evidence.
  • The work would require collecting or handling unnecessary personal data or interacting with an external business system.
