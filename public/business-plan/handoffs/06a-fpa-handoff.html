Handoff 06A — FP&A Model Design

Status: Local draft only Upstream: Product, customer-evidence, and market artifacts through 2026-08-25 Output: working/06-fpa-model-brief.md

Model authorization

Recommended: Build a formula-driven 12-month workbook with downside/base/upside capacity endpoints of 6 / 12 / 24 active workspaces. The workbook can calculate capacity ramp mechanics now; every monetary output must remain blocked with INPUT REQUIRED until price, cost, cash, and timing inputs are sourced.

Non-negotiable financial rules

  • Missing: MAWID price, WTP, conversion, CAC, retention/churn, salaries, vendor costs, hosting, support, tax, cash, and funding are not known.
  • Confirmed: Competitor public list prices are benchmarks only and must not populate MAWID revenue formulas.
  • Recommended: Use symbolic break-even (fixed cost / contribution per workspace) until price and cost inputs are complete.
  • Confirmed: Excluded categories—marketplace, hardware, signage, advertising, payments, POS, payroll, inventory, and custom apps—must not be modeled as initial revenue or cost lines.

Workbook acceptance checks

Recommended: Include a required-input flag, formula-integrity check, scenario capacity reconciliation, non-negative active-workspace check, scope-exclusion check, and competitor-benchmark isolation check. A numeric P&L, cash need, or break-even output is invalid while required inputs are blank.

Decision implication

Recommended: Use the resulting workbook to organize evidence collection and trade-offs, not to claim a financial forecast. Promote inputs only when their owner supplies a documented source or observed pilot record.
