Discovery Coach → Feedback Synthesizer Handoff

Status: Recommended As of: 2026-08-24 (Asia/Beirut) Scope: Local, draft-only transfer specification. No outreach, interview, or external-system action has occurred.

Current evidence state

Missing: No interviews have occurred. There are no customer claims, verbatims, workflow observations, pricing statements, willingness-to-pay statements, authority maps, onboarding findings, language findings, WhatsApp/manual-process findings, cancellation/no-show findings, approval findings, or data/privacy findings to synthesize.

Confirmed: The authoritative product reference describes MAWID as a bilingual English/Arabic private-workspace B2B salon SaaS prototype, with a pending-approval booking workflow and management approve/reject/propose-time actions. It excludes real SMS, payments, backend authentication, POS, payroll, inventory, and a marketplace. Source: mawid-github/README.md.

Recommended: Do not use the product specification as a substitute for customer evidence. It is valid only for product-boundary and concept-description checks.

Evidence expected after interviews

Recommended: After each completed conversation, the Feedback Synthesizer should receive one de-identified, structured note record and atomic evidence items generated from it. Expected fields:

Evidence bundleMinimum contents
Interview metadataRespondent ID; date; segment; broad area; role; decision role; conversation language(s); method; note-taking consent; duration
Current-state workflowA recent concrete booking/cancellation/no-show/reschedule event; intake channels; tools/manual artifacts; people and handoffs; approval and notification steps
Evidence excerptsDe-identified verbatim excerpts or accurate paraphrases separated from interviewer interpretation; evidence type; theme; specificity; direction
Gap mapCurrent state; reported problem; root cause; consequence; desired future state; must-preserve behavior; unresolved question
Buying contextUser, initiator, champion, approver, blocker; trigger/timing; alternatives; evaluation method; onboarding capacity and constraints
Local operating contextArabic/English/RTL needs stated by respondent; WhatsApp/manual-process role; employee selection/availability; cancellation/no-show/approval behavior
Commercial signalsRespondent-specific price-process, payment-basis, and trial-evaluation statements; explicit non-fit; no conversion of reactions into willingness to pay
Risk and privacyHigh-level appointment-data categories, access concerns, retention concerns if stated, and privacy/legal questions; no unnecessary personal data
CodingEvidence label; QUOTE/OBSERVED_WORKFLOW/REPORTED_EVENT/CONCEPT_REACTION/BUYING_PROCESS/CONSTRAINT; theme; S1–S3; SUPPORTS/CHALLENGES/CONDITIONAL/NO_SIGNAL

Required synthesis treatment

Recommended: Build a respondent-by-theme matrix that keeps establishment, segment, role, decision authority, current process, and evidence specificity visible. Count distinct establishments rather than fragments of one conversation. Preserve both supporting and disconfirming evidence.

Recommended: Use material labels as follows:

LabelFeedback Synthesizer use
ConfirmedDirectly supported by a specified source or recorded interview evidence; name the respondent IDs and excerpts/observations behind it
InferredBounded interpretation across the recorded evidence; do not present it as a direct quote or market-wide fact
ConflictingMaterially different accounts by role, establishment, or segment; retain the condition that explains the difference when known
MissingRequired evidence absent, weak, not yet collected, or unable to answer the question
RecommendedNext research, pilot, product, or positioning action; never a claim about what customers have proven

Recommended: Synthesize separately for each segment: current pain, root cause, operational consequence, desired state, buying authority, onboarding burden, constraints, concept relevance, price process, and non-fit. A feature request must be distinct from a validated problem; a positive reaction must be distinct from purchase intent.

What remains unusable before interviews

Missing: Before the first interview, all of the following are unusable as evidence for product, commercial, or investment conclusions:

  • Customer pain, its frequency, and its business consequence.
  • The prevalence or importance of WhatsApp, calls, notebooks, calendars, other software, Arabic/English, or RTL workflow needs.
  • Whether pending approval, rejection, rescheduling, employee selection, cancellation, and no-show flows match real operating practice.
  • Buying authority, champion/blocker mapping, evaluation process, timing, pilot readiness, and onboarding capacity.
  • Willingness to pay, price range, charging basis, setup-fee acceptance, or conversion likelihood.
  • Data/privacy expectations, access constraints, or legal/compliance requirements.
  • Segment priority, market demand, product-market fit, and any forecast, pricing, hiring, or material-investment recommendation that depends on customer proof.

Recommended: Do not infer demand from the interview quota. The recommended sample supports structured qualitative learning only; even completed interviews do not by themselves prove market demand.

Acceptance checklist for a usable post-interview package

Recommended: Mark an interview usable for synthesis only when it contains:

  • A role and segment, with decision-role context.
  • At least one recent, concrete workflow event or an explicit record that no example was available.
  • Evidence and interviewer interpretation stored separately.
  • A data-minimization check showing no unnecessary customer or payment information was retained.
  • At least one question on authority/onboarding and one on constraints, even when the answer is Missing.
  • Evidence codes and a clear follow-up gap.

Recommended: Flag and quarantine any note that includes personal customer data, full message threads, payment data, or claims unsupported by a traceable excerpt/observation. Do not use it until it is minimized and corrected.

Proposed transfer package

Recommended: Transfer a local folder or table containing: (1) de-identified interview notes, (2) atomic coded evidence table, (3) respondent-by-theme matrix, (4) disconfirming-evidence log, (5) segment comparison, (6) Missing-evidence list, and (7) decision-threshold assessment. The Discovery Coach owns question quality and traceability; the Feedback Synthesizer owns theme synthesis, confidence assessment, and feedback-to-product translation.

Missing: There is currently no transfer package beyond this plan because no interviews have been conducted.
