Context

Confirmed: MAWID is a private-workspace, bilingual English/Arabic salon-management and appointment-booking prototype. The testable concept connects a salon-branded customer request with employee availability, manager approval/rejection/proposed time, calendar, and customer-status workflow.

Confirmed: MAWID is not a marketplace. Hardware/signage, advertising, payments, POS, payroll, inventory, and custom per-salon applications are outside the initial plan.

Missing: No customer interviews, customer usage, pilot outcomes, price evidence, sales funnel evidence, operating-cost evidence, legal clearance, or production-readiness evidence exists. The product offer is therefore a discovery hypothesis, not a launch package.

Relevant files:

  • outputs/mawid-business-plan-sprint/05-product-offer-and-packaging.md — authoritative stage-5 scope, hypotheses, measures, and gates.
  • outputs/mawid-business-plan-sprint/04-customer-evidence-and-icp.md — no-evidence finding, provisional cohorts, and evidence-promotion rules.
  • outputs/mawid-business-plan-sprint/03-market-sizing-and-competition.md — public competitor price context only.
  • mawid-github/README.md — authoritative prototype boundary.

Constraints: Local, draft-only work; no external sends or mutations; use only Confirmed, Inferred, Conflicting, Missing, or Recommended for material statements; do not use THE BARBERS as evidence.

Required facts and prohibitions

Confirmed: The only usable product claims are prototype capabilities and scope boundaries cited in mawid-github/README.md.

Confirmed: Fresha, Booksy, and SimplyBook.me public list prices are current official-source context from their recorded pages, but their locale-specific prices are not Lebanese demand or MAWID pricing evidence.

Missing: Customer pain, workflow prevalence, buyer role, onboarding burden, language preference, willingness to pay, price, conversion, CAC, retention, churn, and production readiness.

Recommended: All scenario numbers must be visibly marked as editable hypotheses, tied to a proposed evidence owner and promotion condition.

Pilot gate carried forward

Recommended: Continue only with discovery-oriented definition, research, readiness, and scenario work.

Missing: Do not proceed to a public launch, a price decision, a validated primary ICP claim, a hiring decision, or a financial forecast treated as likely outcome until direct customer, commercial, technical, and legal evidence is collected and reviewed.
